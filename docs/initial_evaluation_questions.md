# Initial RAG Evaluation Questions

These questions will later be used to test retrieval and answer grounding.

## Direct Retrieval

1. What actions are required during the highest operational escalation level?
2. Who should be notified during severe operational pressure?
3. What does the policy state about workforce escalation?
4. Which section covers bed-capacity escalation?
5. What responsibilities are assigned to operational leadership?

## Evidence Synthesis

6. Summarise the main actions required during severe operational pressure.
7. What workforce and capacity actions should be considered together during escalation?
8. What governance responsibilities apply during an operational escalation?

## Insufficient Evidence

9. What medication should be prescribed to patients during operational escalation?
10. What is the clinical treatment for a patient experiencing chest pain?

Expected behaviour for questions 9 and 10:

The system should recognise that the available operational-policy corpus does not provide sufficient evidence for a supported clinical answer.
