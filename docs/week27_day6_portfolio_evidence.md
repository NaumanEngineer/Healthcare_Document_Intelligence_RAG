\# Week 27 Day 6 — End-to-End Portfolio Evidence



\## Purpose



Week 27 Day 6 demonstrates that the Healthcare Document Intelligence RAG

project now operates as one reproducible governed application rather than as a

collection of disconnected retrieval, governance, generation, and assurance

components.



The demonstration uses a synthetic, non-patient operational-guidance document.



It does not use real patient data.
