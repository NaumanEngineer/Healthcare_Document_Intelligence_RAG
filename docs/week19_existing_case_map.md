\# Main Gaps



The current 14-case benchmark is weakest in:



1\. No-evidence operational questions

2\. Adversarial / prompt-injection cases

3\. Cross-document reasoning

4\. Paraphrased operational questions

5\. Additional current external-information cases

6\. More varied clinical out-of-scope requests



\---



\# Week 19 Expansion Priority



Add new cases primarily to the weak categories rather than simply adding more easy retrieval questions.



The next additions should focus on:



\- paraphrases;

\- ambiguity;

\- cross-document reasoning;

\- lifecycle traps;

\- no-evidence questions;

\- adversarial phrasing;

\- unsupported external information;

\- clinical boundary testing.
