Section: Operational Pressure

Operational pressure may occur when emergency demand, workforce constraints, bed occupancy, ambulance handover delays or discharge pressures affect normal service delivery.

Operational leadership should consider several indicators together rather than relying on one metric.

Section: Coordination

During periods of significant pressure, operational teams should coordinate actions across bed management, workforce, emergency care, discharge and site-flow functions.

Actions should be assigned to responsible teams and reviewed as operational conditions change.

This document provides general coordination guidance and does not replace specialist escalation procedures.
