Section: Escalation Triggers

Operational escalation should be considered when system pressure increases across emergency demand, workforce availability, bed capacity and patient flow.

Operational leadership should review escalation indicators and coordinate appropriate actions across relevant services.

Section: Escalation Actions

During significant operational pressure, leadership should review capacity, staffing, discharge arrangements and emergency flow.

Actions should be recorded and reviewed during the period of escalation.
